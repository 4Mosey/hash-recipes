Chapatis

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Roller and rolling pin
  • Sugar and fire source

Steps

  1. Warm your water to mix with he flour
  2. Add sugar and pinched of salt to get the taste
  3. Mix until its harder and cover to simmer
  4. After 30 mins, find your sufuria or pan and put in a source of fire
  5. Pinch and roll each dough into equal sizes, roll into circle shapes
  6. Use cooking oil smeared a little on the fan and turn the chapatis till golden brown and charred.
  7. Voila!!!
